* Summary

This paper proposes MusiSPARQ, a resource control system for the
internet of sounds. Musical metaverse services such as live,
distributed concerts, need to balance two competing execution
strategies: shared resources (SR) and guaranteed resources (GR). The
SPARQ framework optimises routing before runtime whereas MusiSPARQ
allows certain resources to be allocated as SR or GR during runtime,
overcoming some of the potential inefficiencies of SPARQ.

MusiSPARQ is evaluated via a simulated live concert scenario, compared
with all-SR, static premium and max-feasible GR strategies. It is
found that MusiSPARQ performs favourably when compared with other
strategies.

A well-written paper, it appears to show meaningful results that are
of use and interest to the IS2 community. I can therefore recommend it
for inclusion in the proceedings.
